Privacy Policy for RiftTrack

Last updated: March 2026

We DO NOT collect, store, or share ANY personal data

Our Commitment to Privacy

RiftTrack ("we", "our", or "us") is a companion score tracking app for Riftbound TCG. We respect your privacy and have designed our app to function without collecting any personal information whatsoever.

No Data Collection

We want to be completely transparent about our data practices:

  • We do NOT collect any personal information
  • We do NOT track your usage or behavior
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT store any data on external servers
  • We do NOT share any information with third parties
  • We do NOT require account creation or login
  • We do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other device data

Local Storage Only

All game data, player names, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is completely under your control
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

Third-Party Services

RiftTrack fetches champion legend card images from the RiftCodex API, a public Riftbound card database. These requests are made directly from your device and we do not act as an intermediary. We do not control or have access to any data collected by RiftCodex. No personal information is sent in these requests.

Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any data, there are no privacy concerns for children using RiftTrack.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You have complete control over the app and can delete it at any time to remove all locally stored data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us through the App Store or open an issue on our GitHub repository.

This privacy policy is effective as of March 2026.
